Privacy Policy – Grid Drive

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Radder Games (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the mobile game Grid Drive. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, stored, and processed when you use Grid Drive and how that information is used.

1. Information We Collect

Grid Drive does not require you to provide information such as your real name, postal address, or phone number in order to play.

Grid Drive may create a pseudonymous account identifier using Firebase Authentication. This identifier allows game features such as Daily Challenges, scores, rewards, and account linking to be associated with the same player without requiring you to provide a name or email address.

Depending on the features you use, Grid Drive may process information such as:

  • a pseudonymous Firebase user identifier;
  • game progress and gameplay activity;
  • Daily Challenge attempts, scores, number of moves, completion times, and rankings;
  • virtual currency, rewards, lives, and related gameplay state;
  • notification preferences and notification delivery information;
  • device and app information;
  • diagnostic and crash information;
  • advertising-related information processed by Google AdMob; and
  • Google Play Games account information when you voluntarily link your Google Play Games account.

2. Firebase Authentication

Grid Drive uses Firebase Authentication to provide a persistent, pseudonymous identity for certain online features.

By default, Grid Drive may use anonymous Firebase Authentication. This creates a unique account identifier without requiring you to provide an email address, password, or other contact information.

This identifier may be used to associate Daily Challenge attempts, leaderboard results, rewards, notification registrations, and other server-side game data with the correct player.

3. Google Play Games

Grid Drive allows Android players to optionally link their Google Play Games account to their existing Grid Drive account.

Linking Google Play Games is optional. If you choose to link your account, Google may provide Grid Drive with identifiers and profile information made available through Google Play Games, such as your Google Play Games player identifier and available profile information.

Grid Drive uses this information to connect your Google Play Games identity with your existing Grid Drive account. Linking is designed to keep the same Grid Drive account and gameplay identity rather than creating a separate game account.

Google Play Games information is processed according to Google's own terms and privacy policies.

4. Daily Challenges and Leaderboards

Grid Drive provides online Daily Challenges and leaderboard features.

When you participate in a Daily Challenge, information associated with your pseudonymous player account may be stored on our Firebase backend, including:

  • the Daily Challenge date;
  • whether an attempt was started or completed;
  • your score;
  • the number of moves used;
  • completion time;
  • final leaderboard rank;
  • reward eligibility; and
  • technical information needed to validate and settle the result.

Daily Challenge rankings may be displayed to other players as part of the leaderboard. Grid Drive may also include simulated leaderboard entries for gameplay presentation. Simulated entries are not real users and are not eligible for player rewards.

Certain Daily Challenge rewards may be granted based on final ranking. These rewards are associated with your pseudonymous Grid Drive account.

5. Friends and Social Comparison

Grid Drive may allow players who have linked Google Play Games to compare Daily Challenge results with their Google Play Games friends.

If you choose to use a friends feature, Google Play Games may ask for your permission before providing friend-related information.

Grid Drive may use Google Play Games player identifiers to determine which players are also your Google Play Games friends and to display relevant Grid Drive scores or leaderboard results.

We do not need your friends' email addresses, phone numbers, or other contact information for this feature.

Access to friend information depends on Google Play Games availability, permissions, and privacy settings. You may be able to manage those settings through your Google Play Games or Google account settings.

6. Analytics

Grid Drive uses Google Firebase Analytics to help us understand how the game is used and how we can improve it.

Analytics collection is optional in Grid Drive. When first using the app, you can choose whether to share anonymous usage data. If you decline, Firebase Analytics collection is disabled.

If enabled, analytics information may include information about game sessions and interactions, such as screens viewed, levels started or completed, hints used, Daily Challenge activity, and other gameplay events, together with device and app information.

You can change your analytics preference later from the game's Settings menu.

7. Advertising

Grid Drive uses Google AdMob to provide advertisements, including rewarded advertisements and interstitial advertisements.

Google Mobile Ads may process information such as IP address, advertising or device identifiers, interactions with advertisements and the app, and diagnostic information. This information may be used for advertising, analytics, security, and fraud prevention.

Where required, Grid Drive uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain and manage advertising consent. Available privacy choices can be reviewed again through the Privacy option in the game's Settings menu.

Depending on your location and consent choices, Google may provide personalized, non-personalized, limited, or otherwise legally permitted advertising.

8. Crash Reporting

Grid Drive uses Firebase Crashlytics to detect crashes and technical problems and improve the stability of the game.

Crash reports may contain technical and diagnostic information about the app, device, operating system, app version, and circumstances surrounding a crash.

9. Notifications

Grid Drive may offer optional notifications for game-related events. Depending on your preferences, these may include notifications about:

  • a new Daily Challenge being available;
  • your Daily Challenge result being ready;
  • your lives being fully restored; and
  • free rewarded-coin videos becoming available again.

Notifications require permission from your device operating system. You can enable or disable supported notification categories from the Grid Drive Settings menu and can also manage notification permission from your device settings.

Grid Drive uses Firebase Cloud Messaging for certain server-based notifications, such as Daily Challenge notifications.

To send player-specific notifications, such as a Daily Challenge result, Grid Drive may store a Firebase Cloud Messaging device token associated with your pseudonymous Firebase user identifier. Notification registration may also include technical information such as your device's time zone or UTC offset so that notifications can be delivered at an appropriate time.

Lives and free-coin availability notifications may be scheduled locally on your device and do not require your gameplay state to be sent to a server for the purpose of scheduling those notifications.

10. How We Use Information

Information processed through Grid Drive and the services described in this policy may be used to:

  • operate and maintain Grid Drive;
  • provide game progress and online game functionality;
  • authenticate and identify pseudonymous player accounts;
  • link an optional Google Play Games account;
  • operate Daily Challenges and leaderboards;
  • calculate rankings and distribute eligible game rewards;
  • allow supported social and friends comparisons;
  • deliver notifications you have chosen to receive;
  • understand how players use the game when analytics has been enabled;
  • improve gameplay, usability, performance, and stability;
  • diagnose crashes and technical problems;
  • display and measure advertisements; and
  • prevent fraud, cheating, abuse, and unauthorized use.

11. Third-Party Services

Grid Drive uses services provided by Google, including:

  • Google Play Games Services;
  • Google Firebase Authentication;
  • Google Cloud Firestore and Firebase backend services;
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging;
  • Google Firebase Analytics;
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics;
  • Google AdMob; and
  • Google User Messaging Platform.

These services may process information according to Google's own terms, privacy policies, and applicable service configurations.

12. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required, Grid Drive presents Google's consent interface for advertising-related privacy choices.

Analytics is handled separately from advertising consent. You can choose whether to share analytics usage data with us, and you can change this preference through the Settings menu.

Notification preferences can also be managed through the Grid Drive Settings menu. Device-level notification permission can be changed through your operating system settings.

Linking Google Play Games is optional. Features that specifically rely on Google Play Games, such as certain social or friends features, may be unavailable if you choose not to link your account or do not grant the required Google Play Games permissions.

Advertising privacy choices can also be accessed through the Privacy option in Settings when such options are available.

13. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by Google and Firebase through the third-party services described above for purposes such as authentication, cloud game functionality, notifications, advertisements, analytics, diagnostics, service operation, security, and fraud prevention.

Leaderboard-related gameplay information may be displayed to other Grid Drive players as part of the game's leaderboard and social functionality.

14. Data Retention and Deletion

Grid Drive does not require you to create an account using your name, email address, or phone number. However, a pseudonymous Firebase account identifier and associated online game data may be retained in order to provide Daily Challenges, rankings, rewards, notifications, account linking, and related online features.

Server-side gameplay records may be retained for purposes including leaderboard history, reward validation, fraud prevention, diagnostics, and operation of the game.

Notification tokens may be updated, disabled, or removed when they are no longer valid or when the relevant notification preference is disabled.

Data processed by third-party services may also be retained according to the applicable service provider's retention policies and our configuration of those services.

You can disable future optional analytics collection from the game's Settings menu. You can also review advertising privacy choices and notification preferences through Settings.

For requests relating to access, correction, or deletion of information under our control, contact us using the email address below.

Depending on the type of request, we may need information that allows us to identify the relevant pseudonymous game account without requiring unnecessary personal information.

15. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through Grid Drive.

Information transmitted between the app and Google or Firebase services is protected in transit using appropriate security measures such as encryption. Access to server-side game data is restricted according to the technical requirements of the game and backend services.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can, however, be guaranteed to be completely secure.

16. Children's Privacy

Grid Drive is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children beyond information that may be processed as part of the game and the third-party services described in this policy.

Advertising, consent, account, social, and data-processing practices may depend on the age groups for which the app is made available and the applicable Google Play, Google Play Games, Firebase, and Google advertising requirements.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Grid Drive, the services it uses, or applicable requirements change.

The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
