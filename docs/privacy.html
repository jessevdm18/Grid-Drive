Privacy Policy – Grid Drive

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Radder Games (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates the mobile game Grid Drive. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected and processed when you use Grid Drive.

1. Information We Collect

Grid Drive does not require you to create an account and does not ask you to provide information such as your name, address, or phone number.

The app uses third-party services that may automatically process certain device and usage information.

2. Analytics

Grid Drive uses Google Firebase Analytics to help us understand how the game is used and how we can improve it.

Analytics collection is optional in Grid Drive. When first using the app, you can choose whether to share anonymous usage data. If you decline, Firebase Analytics collection is disabled.

If enabled, analytics information may include information about game sessions and interactions, such as screens viewed, levels started or completed, hints used, and other gameplay events, together with device and app information.

You can change your analytics preference later from the game's Settings menu.

3. Advertising

Grid Drive uses Google AdMob to provide advertisements, including rewarded advertisements and interstitial advertisements.

Google Mobile Ads may process information such as IP address, advertising or device identifiers, interactions with advertisements and the app, and diagnostic information. This information may be used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.

Where required, Grid Drive uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain and manage advertising consent. Available privacy choices can be reviewed again through the Privacy option in the game's Settings menu.

Depending on your location and consent choices, Google may provide personalized, non-personalized, limited, or otherwise legally permitted advertising.

4. Crash Reporting

Grid Drive uses Firebase Crashlytics to detect crashes and technical problems and improve the stability of the game. Crash reports may contain technical and diagnostic information about the app, device, and circumstances surrounding a crash.

5. How We Use Information

Information processed through these services may be used to:

  • operate and maintain Grid Drive;
  • understand how players use the game when analytics has been enabled;
  • improve gameplay, usability, performance, and stability;
  • diagnose crashes and technical problems;
  • display and measure advertisements; and
  • prevent fraud and abuse.

6. Third-Party Services

Grid Drive uses services provided by Google, including:

  • Google AdMob;
  • Google User Messaging Platform;
  • Google Firebase Analytics; and
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics.

These services may process data according to Google's own terms and privacy policies.

7. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required, Grid Drive presents Google's consent interface for advertising-related privacy choices.

Analytics is handled separately from advertising consent. You can choose whether to share analytics usage data with us, and you can change this preference through the Settings menu.

Advertising privacy choices can also be accessed through the Privacy option in Settings when such options are available.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by Google through the third-party services described above for purposes such as providing advertisements, analytics, diagnostics, service operation, security, and fraud prevention.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through Grid Drive. Data transmitted by the Google services used by the app is protected in transit using appropriate security measures such as encryption.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can, however, be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Grid Drive itself does not require a user account or maintain an account-based profile containing your name or contact details.

Data processed by third-party services may be retained according to the applicable service provider's retention policies and our configuration of those services.

You can disable future optional analytics collection from the game's Settings menu. You can also review available advertising privacy choices through the Privacy option.

For privacy or deletion requests relating to information under our control, contact us using the email address below.

11. Children's Privacy

Grid Drive is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising, consent, and data-processing practices may depend on the age groups for which the app is made available and the applicable Google Play and Google advertising requirements.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Grid Drive, the services it uses, or applicable requirements change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
